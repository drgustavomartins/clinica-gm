Our Treatments

Procedures

Botulinum Toxin (Botox)

Smooths wrinkles and prevents new signs of aging, providing a rested and rejuvenated appearance without losing your natural look. Average duration: 4 to 6 months.

Dermal Fillers

Restores volume and redefines facial contours, providing a youthful and balanced appearance without excess. Duration: 12 to 24 months.

Collagen Biostimulators

Activates natural collagen production, restoring firmness, elasticity and glow to your skin in a progressive and long-lasting way. Stimulates collagen for up to 2 years.

Thread Lift

Provides an immediate lifting effect and stimulates collagen, leaving your skin firmer and rejuvenated without the need for surgery.

iSkinTech

Protocol combining deep cleansing and revitalization, leaving your skin more uniform, hydrated and with a healthy glow.

Lip Augmentation

Enhances contour, corrects asymmetries and restores hydration, ensuring a sophisticated and balanced result.

Exclusive Protocol
NaturalUp®

The evolution and management of your aging process can work in your favor over time.

NaturalUp® is an exclusive protocol created by Dr. Gustavo Martins that integrates different techniques for natural and progressive rejuvenation. Instead of correcting punctually, the method treats continuously, respecting each patient's biology.

01

Structural Support

We restore support and projection to deep facial tissues with state-of-the-art biostimulators.

02

Global Stimulation

We activate natural collagen production throughout the face, progressively improving texture and firmness.

03

Smart Toxin

Strategic application to soften expression marks while preserving natural movement.

04

Targeted Filling

Volume and definition where it really matters. Every millimeter planned for harmony and balance.

05

Tissue Regeneration

Autologous and regenerative materials that activate your body's natural ability to repair itself. Biology in your favor.
